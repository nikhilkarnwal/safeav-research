Research direction

Reliability across
the driving pipeline.

The current study investigates calibrated multi-hazard safety supervision in CARLA, comparing separate collision and red-light constraints with a weighted risk score.

The broader interests span scene understanding, prediction and world models, planning and control, and generalization. These provide a home for future projects as their scope and evidence develop.

Research principles

How we evaluate an idea.

Make the comparison fair

Share predictors, interventions, scenario configurations, and seeds. Tune baselines carefully and compare driving utility alongside safety.

Make failures visible

Report individual hazards and scenario families. Examine disagreements, late interventions, and unnecessary braking.

Make experiments reproducible

Connect results to configurations, software versions, split manifests, checkpoints, and recorded decisions.

Keep claims proportional to evidence

Distinguish plans from findings, simulation from real-world validation, and empirical thresholds from formal guarantees.
